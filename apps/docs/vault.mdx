The Vault is where your workspace keeps the accounts and keys its coworkers share: the apps you signed in to, the secrets you added and the MCP servers you registered. You sign in to an app once, then switch it on for the coworkers that need it, or for every coworker.

Values never leave your workspace's machine and are never shown again once saved, in the Vault or anywhere else. See [Where credentials live](/agents/apps#where-credentials-live).

## Open the Vault

Click the workspace pill in the top left corner, then **Vault**. An orange dot next to it means an app of the Vault is signed out.

The Vault lives on your workspace's machine. On Rerun Cloud, the entry stays disabled until your free trial starts.

## What the Vault holds

| Entry | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Apps** | Apps of the library you signed in to, shown as tiles with their logo, and the accounts of paid apps |
| **MCP server** | A server of your own, by address or by command |
| **Secret** | A key or a token your coworkers read as a variable |

Each entry says who uses it: **Every coworker**, the names of the coworkers, or **No coworker yet**. From 8 entries on, a search field appears on top.

<Shot src="/images/vault/vault-list" alt="The Vault with three apps, Gmail, HubSpot and Stripe, and a secret, each tile showing the coworkers that use it" />

### Statuses

Most entries show no badge: they are connected and their coworkers use them. Two badges ask for you:

| Badge | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Signed out** | Its coworkers can't use the app until someone signs in again. Open the entry and click **Reconnect** |
| **Not set up** | The app is waiting for a credential. Open the entry and fill in its form |

For some apps whose sign-in Rerun handles for you, the Vault cannot sign them in again itself: the entry says **Reconnect it from the Apps tab of a coworker that uses it**.

## Add to the Vault

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the add page">
    Click **Add** at the top of the Vault. In an empty Vault, click one of the popular apps, or **Other app**, **MCP server** or **Secret**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick what to add">
    Under **Add to the Vault**, choose **Apps**, **MCP server** or **Secret**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in or fill the form">
    For an app, search it with **Search apps**, then sign in or paste its key. In the [desktop app](/desktop-app), the sign-in opens in your browser and the app picks it up once you approve. LinkedIn Pro and WhatsApp Pro come first in the list and open their own window, where you connect the account and its slot: the account then opens in the Vault. See the forms below for a server or a secret.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick its coworkers">
    The entry opens once it is added: switch it on for the coworkers that should use it. See [Share an entry](#share-an-entry).
  </Step>
</Steps>

Some apps can only be connected from a coworker: the Vault then says **This app can't be added to the Vault yet. Connect it from a coworker's Apps tab.** It lands in the Vault anyway, see [From a coworker](#from-a-coworker).

Any app with an account can be added several times, one entry per account, and a coworker can use them all: it goes by the name of each account, and asks you which one when several fit. The accounts of the [paid apps](#paid-apps) are connected in their own window. The second one is named after the app and a number, like `GitHub 2`, until its sign-in reports an email or a workspace name. To tell them apart, [rename them](#rename-an-account).

### Add a secret

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | What people read, like `Stripe live key` |
| **Variable** | The name coworkers read it under, like `STRIPE_LIVE_KEY`. It is filled from the name as you type: letters, digits and underscores, not starting with a digit |
| **Value** | The secret itself. It is never shown again |

Click **Add secret**.

### Add an MCP server

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | What people read, like `Company docs` |
| **Address or command** | An `https` address, or the command the machine starts |
| **Header** and **Value** | Optional: a header the server needs, like `Authorization`, and its value. The value is kept as a variable of the entry |

Click **Add server**. A remote server with no key offers to sign in from its entry: open it, click **Reconnect**, then **Connect**. A coworker that needs the sign-in sends you there, it cannot start it from the chat. See [what a server of your own needs](/agents/apps#missing-an-app) to sign in by OAuth.

To copy a server's JSON config instead, click **or paste a JSON config** under **Address or command** and paste it, or paste it straight into **Name** or **Address or command**. The fields fill in, with one line per header and per environment variable: check each value and change it before adding, they are kept as variables of the entry. Change the address or the command to another server's and those lines go away. A config with several servers adds one entry per server with **Add N servers**. If one fails, the ones before it are already in the Vault and the form keeps the rest. The formats accepted are the same as [for a coworker](/agents/apps#missing-an-app).

## Share an entry

Open an entry to see its **Coworkers**:

<Loop src="/images/vault/vault-share-entry" alt="The Gmail entry of the Vault, where switching on Invoice Chaser gives it the app" />

- Switch on **Every coworker** to give it to the whole workspace. Coworkers added later get it too.
- Or switch it on coworker by coworker.

A coworker cannot get the same variable or the same server name from two entries: the Vault refuses with **already gets {name} from another Vault entry**. An account added while its app took a single account shares its names with the first one: delete it and add it again to give it names of its own.

The entry also lists the names of its **Variables**, never their values.

### Rename an account

Open an app's entry and click the pencil next to its name, at the top. Type a name, up to 60 characters, like `Acme org` or `Personal`, then press Enter or click elsewhere. Escape cancels.

The name replaces the account line under the app's name everywhere: on its tile, in a coworker's **Apps** tab, on the **Use** button of an app card in the chat, and in what coworkers read about their apps. Signing in to the account again keeps it.

To go back to the name the account had, empty the field and press Enter: the field shows that name while it is empty.

### Delete an entry

Click **Delete** at the bottom of an entry. The confirmation names the coworkers that lose it.

## Ask the Chief of Staff

The [Chief of Staff](/chief-of-staff) manages the Vault for you. Ask it "what is in the Vault?", "give the Gmail account to Lea" or "take Notion back from the Support coworker": it reads the entries, lends them, takes them back and renames an account, with your rights. Ask it to "add Notion to the Vault" or "add our docs server": it adds the entry, then opens the Vault on it so you sign in or type the key yourself. It does the same for an entry whose sign-in expired. Ask it to "add our OpenAI key to the Vault and give it to Lea": it shows a masked form, and the key you type goes straight to the Vault, lent to Lea, without ever entering the chat. The [iPhone app](/iphone-app) does not show that form yet: it links to the conversation on the web. It asks you before it deletes an entry, naming the coworkers that lose it.

## From a coworker

Apps you install from a coworker's **Apps** tab go to the Vault too, switched on for that coworker only. So do the apps a coworker is given when it is created, or that an AI assistant attaches to it through the [Rerun API](/api/introduction): they wait in the Vault until you connect them, unless they need nothing to connect. Paid apps stay with the coworker. Open the Vault to hand them to other coworkers.

In a coworker's **Apps** tab:

- An app, server or secret lent by the Vault carries a **Vault** badge. Removing it only takes it away from that coworker: it stays in the Vault, for the other coworkers and for later. An entry shared with every coworker cannot be removed there: **Every coworker gets it from the Vault. Change it there.**
- **From the Vault** lists every entry the coworker does not have yet, each with **Add**.

When a coworker suggests an app in the chat and the Vault already holds an account of it, the card offers **Use** that account in one click, or **New account**.

The [iPhone app](/iphone-app) offers the same: in that card, in the card of an app the coworker added, and on an app of a coworker's **Apps** screen that needs a sign-in. The coworker gets the account at once and the conversation goes on. Only a member who manages the coworker's Box can lend it an account; the others see why under the button. A coworker whose own copy of the app still waits on its sign-in drops that copy when it is lent an account, so the app reads as connected. The copy leaves the Vault too when no other coworker holds it and nobody ever signed in to it; otherwise it stays there for an admin to delete.

A secret a coworker asks you for in the chat, and an MCP server it registers itself, stay with that coworker: they do not go to the Vault. The Chief of Staff holds entries like a coworker: an admin switches it on in the entry's **Coworkers** list, where it comes first, or connects an app from the **Apps** screen of its card, and an entry shared with every coworker reaches it too. A paid app account cannot be attached to it.

See [Apps](/agents/apps).

## From the API

An AI assistant connected to your workspace through the [Rerun API](/api/introduction) reads the Vault with `list_vault`: each entry, whether it works and which coworkers get it, never a value. It switches an entry on or off for a coworker with `attach_vault_entry` and `detach_vault_entry`, and for every coworker with `share_vault_entry`, and deletes one with `delete_vault_entry`. Adding an entry and typing its credentials stay in the app. See [Vault tools](/api/tools/vault).

## Paid apps

The accounts of paid apps, like LinkedIn Pro and WhatsApp Pro, show in the Vault with a **Paid** badge, and **Paused** or **Connecting** while they are not ready. They are attached coworker by coworker: **Every coworker** stays off (**A paid app is attached coworker by coworker**). A coworker can have several accounts of the same paid app: switching one on adds it to those it has, and switching one off leaves the others and their wake-ups as they are. Their sign-in, slots and billing stay in the app's own window. See [Plans and billing](/billing).

## Who can do what

The Vault follows the roles and the Box access of [Teams and workspaces](/teams).

| Action | Who |
| --- | --- |
| Open the Vault | Every member |
| Add an entry | A member who manages at least one Box (**Manage a Box to add to the Vault**) |
| Switch an entry on or off for a coworker | A member who manages that coworker's Box (**You don't manage this coworker's Box**) |
| Change an entry other coworkers use, rename an account | An admin, or a member who manages the Box of every coworker that uses it |
| Switch on **Every coworker**, change or rename an entry shared with every coworker, delete an entry | An admin (**Only an admin can do this**) |

A member only sees the coworkers of the Boxes they can open. The coworkers of the other Boxes are counted, not named: an entry says **2 coworkers in Boxes you can't see**, and so does the confirmation before a delete.
