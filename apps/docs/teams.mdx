## Workspaces

A workspace is your account: one plan, one machine, one set of members, and the Boxes and coworkers they share.

You can belong to several. Switch between them from the pill in the top left corner, which also creates new ones.

A new workspace opens on its own onboarding. Until it is done, the same pill sits at the top of every onboarding step, the waitlist included, so you can go back to your other workspaces.

A workspace is a hard boundary. Nothing crosses it: not a coworker, not the shared database, not an [API key](/api/authentication). That makes a second workspace the right tool when you want a real separation, for example between a client project and your own.

## Invite someone

Only an admin can invite.

<Shot src="/images/teams/members" alt="The Members dialog: the admin of the workspace, and the Invite by email field with its Boxes picker" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the members">
    Click the workspace pill, then **Members**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter an email address">
    Type it under **Invite by email**, then pick the **Boxes** they reach: **All Boxes**, every Box open to the workspace, or **None, assign later**, only the Boxes you give them afterwards. Click **Invite**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They accept">
    The invitation link is valid for 7 days and works once, for that email only. They join as a **Member** and see the Boxes you gave them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On Rerun Cloud the invitation goes out by email. A self-hosted install that sends no email shows the link instead: click **Copy the invitation link** and pass it on yourself.

Pending invitations are listed under the members, and an admin can revoke one before it is accepted. Inviting the same address again keeps the same link.

You can also ask the [Chief of Staff](/chief-of-staff) to invite them: it asks you for the addresses, shows them back to you, and only sends the invitations you confirm, with the same choice between every Box and the ones you give them later. Only an admin can.

Each member takes a seat. Neither Pro nor the free trial limits seats. See [Plans and billing](/billing).

## Roles

| Role | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Admin** | Manages billing, members, Box access, the workspace settings, API keys, AI providers and paid apps. Reaches every Box |
| **Member** | Uses the coworkers of the Boxes they reach. This is what an invited person becomes |

An admin changes a role from the member's row in **Members**: **Make admin** or **Make member**. The workspace always keeps at least one admin, so the last one cannot be made a member.

## Access by Box

Every Box is open to the whole workspace until an admin restricts it. Right-click a Box on the board and pick **Manage access**:

- **Who can use this Box**: **Everyone in the workspace**, or **Only selected people**.
- **People**: the teammates named on the Box, each with a level. **Can use** chats with its coworkers and sees their work. **Can manage** also edits, moves and deletes them.

Restricting a Box keeps the people already named on it and the admins. Admins always have access to every Box. The person who created a Box is named on it with **Can manage**.

From **Members**, an admin also sees one person's Boxes and decides whether they use every Box open to everyone or only the Boxes named to them.

## What members share

Everyone in a workspace sees the same Boxes they reach and the same coworkers in them. That includes the conversations coworkers have had and the data they hold.

Credentials are not readable by anyone, member or not: they live on the workspace machine and are never displayed back. But a coworker connected to your Gmail can be asked to act on it by anyone who can use its Box, so give access accordingly.

## Leaving and removing

An admin removes a member from the same **Members** dialog. Removing someone frees their seat immediately and takes away their access to everything in the workspace. You can invite them back later.

You cannot remove yourself, and the last admin cannot be removed. To hand the workspace over, make someone else an admin first.

In a workspace an organization runs for you, **Remove** is off: ask the organization to remove someone.

## Languages

Rerun speaks English, Français, Español, Português, Deutsch, Türkçe, العربية, 简体中文 (Simplified Chinese), 繁體中文 (台灣) (Traditional Chinese, Taiwan) and עברית. In Arabic and Hebrew the whole interface reads right to left.

- **Your language.** Open the workspace pill, then **Account**, then the **Language** tab. **Automatic** follows your browser. It is the language Rerun uses with you, and it is yours alone.
- **Coworkers' language.** Open the workspace pill, then **Settings**. It is what your coworkers write in when they work on their own, on a schedule or a trigger. Only an admin can change it.

## Account security

Two-step verification (an authenticator app, passkeys and backup codes) and the list of where you are signed in live in the **Security** tab of **Account**. See [Account security](/account-security).

## Delete your account

Open the workspace pill in the top left corner, then **Account**, then the **Delete account** tab. You type `DELETE` to confirm (or click it to copy it, then paste it), and you are signed out.

Deleting your account:

- Takes you out of every workspace you share with other members. They keep the workspace, its coworkers and its files.
- Stops for good every workspace only you are in: its coworkers are turned off and its machine is deleted with its files. Its [backups](/concepts#backups) cannot be deleted early: they expire on their own, within 30 days.
- Frees your email address, your Google account and your phone number. You can sign up again later, as a new account that starts empty.

It cannot be undone.

If a workspace only you are in has a subscription that still renews, cancel it first from **Billing**. The **Delete account** tab names the workspaces in the way. A trial without a card has nothing to cancel.
