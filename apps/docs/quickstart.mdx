## Before you start

Open [app.rerun.build](https://app.rerun.build). Rerun starts with a 5 day free trial.

Nothing to install. Everything happens in the browser.

<Note>
  This page follows Rerun Cloud. On a self-hosted install, see [Install](/self-host/install).
</Note>

## 1. Create your account

Every page of Rerun asks for an account first. Sign in or sign up with Google, Apple, your email or your phone number: the email and the phone get a 6-digit code. After a Google or Apple sign-up, Rerun asks for your phone number once; **Skip for now** goes on without it.

Everything up to the Chief of Staff happens in one window, open over the app.

## 2. Tell Rerun about your company

Four questions, one at a time:

1. **What's your website?** Rerun reads it to learn what you sell and who you sell to. A LinkedIn page or another platform is not your site: enter your own, or click **I don't have one**.
2. **What's your line of work?** Pick a tile. The one your website was read as is already picked. None fits? **Other** takes it in your own words.
3. **What do you do there?** Pick your role, or **Something else** to type it.
4. **Invite your team.** Enter an address and click **Invite**: the invitation leaves at once. Add as many as you like, or **Skip**. See [Teams](/teams).

<Shot src="/images/quickstart/website-question" alt="The first onboarding question, What's your website?, in a window over the app" />

Each answer is saved the moment you give it: reload or come back tomorrow, and you pick up at the next question.

Came through a link to a template or a shared coworker? You answer the same questions, and what you came for opens once you are in.

## 3. Start your trial

The checkout takes the window's place: what the trial includes, what is due today, and when the first charge comes. Enter your card and click **Start my free trial**. Closed it too soon? **Start my free trial** in the window opens it again. See [The trial](/billing#the-trial) for the card and the dates.

## 4. Meet your Chief of Staff

The moment the trial is on, your workspace gets its machine, a private computer in the cloud, isolated from every other account, that hosts every coworker you build. The window shows each step as it happens, then the [Chief of Staff](/chief-of-staff), the assistant of your workspace, wakes up. Click **Let's go**.

The Chief of Staff speaks first: what it understood of your company, and the first coworkers it would hire for your line of work, like "Book more demos" or "Chase my unpaid invoices". Pick one and it installs it on the board. None of them fits? Tell it what you need, and it designs that coworker. Along the way it offers to take a name of your choosing: see [Its name](/chief-of-staff#its-name).

At the same time, Rerun reads your website into the [Brain](/brain): the pages about your company every coworker reads before it works, with what you sell, to whom, your team, your voice and your tools. It shows up within a few minutes.

## 5. Set up each coworker

A new coworker still has to be connected to your own accounts. Until it is, its chat opens on a card instead of a message box: **Set up** and the coworker's name, with the number of apps to connect and a time estimate.

- **Install** and the coworker's name starts the setup.
- **Continue setup** appears when the setup already runs in another conversation, and takes you there.
- **Skip for now** gives you the message box. The coworker is not set up, so it may lack what it needs.

For the first coworker you picked, the Chief of Staff also offers **Start installing** with its name. The others wait in their own chat, whenever you like.

The setup is a conversation: the coworker tells you what it needs, offers to connect the apps it is missing, and asks you for the values only you can give.

<Note>
  When it needs an API key, the coworker opens a masked form. The value goes straight to your machine and is never shown to the coworker or stored in the conversation. Never paste a secret into the chat itself.
</Note>

When the setup is done, the coworker switches on the routines and webhooks it shipped with, and tells you what it can do and when it will next run.

## 6. Talk to it

You now have a normal message box. Ask it for something.

<Shot src="/images/quickstart/coworker-chat" alt="Lead Scout's chat in the Simple view: the request, one step, and five prospects added to HubSpot" />

When it has to look something up or act before answering, it first tells you in a sentence where it starts, and on long work it drops a line each time it finds something or moves on. While it works you see every step: the files it reads, the searches it runs, the tools it calls. If it needs a decision, it stops and asks you, right in the chat.

## 7. Finish the Getting started checklist

The **Getting started** checklist lists what makes a workspace run on its own: **Create your first coworker**, **Plug in your AI subscription**, **Invite a teammate**, **Schedule a recurring task** and **Add a payment method**, plus **Verify your phone number** when your sign-up asked for one. Click a step and the Chief of Staff walks you through it, or opens the right settings.

Complete every step during the trial and it gets 3 more days. See [Getting started](/chief-of-staff#getting-started).

## Let it run without you

Two ways to make a coworker work while you are away.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Routines" icon="clock" href="/agents/automations">
    A schedule and an instruction. Every Monday at 9, summarise last week and email it to me.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Webhook triggers" icon="webhook" href="/api/webhooks">
    A public URL. When Stripe posts a refund, handle it.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

You can add both from the coworker's **Routines** screen, or just ask the coworker to set them up for you.
