## Before you start

Open [app.rerun.build](https://app.rerun.build) and sign in with your phone number, Google, Apple or your email. Rerun starts with a 5 day free trial.

Nothing to install. Everything happens in the browser.

<Note>
  This page follows Rerun Cloud. On a self-hosted install, see [Install](/self-host/install).
</Note>

## 1. Tell Rerun who you are

Your workspace opens on a short setup, one question at a time. It stays open until the setup is done; **Sign out** leaves it.

1. **What is your company website?** Rerun reads it to learn what you sell and who you sell to. A LinkedIn page or another platform is not your site: enter your own, or click **Skip**.
2. **What line of work are you in?** Only asked when you skipped the website, which answers it on its own.
3. **And what do you do there?** It decides who gets built for you first.
4. **Your first name?** Only asked when Rerun does not know it yet, for example from a Google sign-in.
5. **Your phone number**, so Rerun can reach you when a coworker needs you. Only asked when your sign-in did not give it.

## 2. Invite your team

Enter the email addresses of up to 10 teammates, or click **Skip**. They get their invite once your team is built. You can invite more from **Members** later.

## 3. Start your trial

Your team works on a machine of its own, which the trial opens. Click **Continue** to start it. See [The trial](/billing#the-trial) for the card and the dates.

The moment the trial is on, your workspace gets its machine, a private computer in the cloud, isolated from every other account, that hosts every coworker you build. When every machine is taken, the setup shows **Your machine is on its way** and moves on by itself as soon as one frees up.

At the same time, Rerun reads your website into the [Brain](/brain): the pages about your company every coworker reads before it works, with what you sell, to whom, your team, your voice and your tools. It shows up within a few minutes.

## 4. Pick your starter team

The setup closes and the [Chief of Staff](/chief-of-staff) takes over, on your machine. It says what it understood about your company and offers **Your starter team**: ready-made coworkers for your line of work, each sold on what it does for you, like "Book more meetings" or "Chase my unpaid invoices". Pick up to 3, the number of coworkers the trial runs, then click **Hire this one**, or **Hire these 3** when you picked several.

None of them fits? Tell the Chief of Staff what you need instead: it designs that coworker with you and builds it once you agree.

Your team is built on the board, in Boxes named after your line of work, and the invitations of step 2 leave.

<Note>
  You opened Rerun from a template or a shared coworker? The setup skips the questions and the invitations, and installs it on your board once the machine is there. The Chief of Staff then picks up from it.
</Note>

## 5. Set up each coworker

A new coworker still has to be connected to your own accounts. Until it is, its chat opens on a card instead of a message box: **Set up** and the coworker's name, with the number of apps to connect and a time estimate.

- **Install** and the coworker's name starts the setup.
- **Continue setup** appears when the setup already runs in another conversation, and takes you there.
- **Skip for now** gives you the message box. The coworker is not set up, so it may lack what it needs.

For the first coworker you picked, the Chief of Staff also offers **Start installing** with its name. The others wait in their own chat, whenever you like.

The setup is a conversation: the coworker tells you what it needs, offers to connect the apps it is missing, and asks you for the values only you can give.

<Note>
  When it needs an API key, the coworker opens a masked form. The value goes straight to your machine and is never shown to the coworker or stored in the conversation. Never paste a secret into the chat itself.
</Note>

When the setup is done, the coworker switches on the routines and webhooks it shipped with, and tells you what it can do and when it will next run.

## 6. Talk to it

You now have a normal message box. Ask it for something.

While it works you see every step: the files it reads, the searches it runs, the tools it calls. If it needs a decision, it stops and asks you, right in the chat.

## 7. Finish the Getting started checklist

The **Getting started** checklist lists what makes a workspace run on its own: **Create your first coworker**, **Plug in your AI subscription**, **Invite a teammate**, **Schedule a recurring task** and **Add a payment method**, plus **Verify your phone number** when your sign-up asked for one. Click a step and the Chief of Staff walks you through it, or opens the right settings.

Complete every step during the trial and it gets 3 more days. See [Getting started](/chief-of-staff#getting-started).

## Let it run without you

Two ways to make a coworker work while you are away.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Routines" icon="clock" href="/agents/automations">
    A schedule and an instruction. Every Monday at 9, summarise last week and email it to me.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Webhook triggers" icon="webhook" href="/api/webhooks">
    A public URL. When Stripe posts a refund, handle it.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

You can add both from the coworker's **Routines** screen, or just ask the coworker to set them up for you.
