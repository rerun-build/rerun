The Rerun app for iPhone (iOS) opens the same workspace as the web app. Your coworkers, your conversations and your plan are the same on both, and a message sent from one shows up on the other.

## Install

Get Rerun from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6825274993) and open it.

## Sign in

Sign in the way you sign in on the web: **Continue with Apple**, **Continue with Google**, **Continue with phone** (a code by WhatsApp or SMS) or **Continue with email** (a code by email). Use the same account as on the web to find your workspace.

## What you can do

- **Talk to your coworkers**: the home screen lists them with what each one is doing. Tap one to open its conversation, send a message, a photo, a file or a voice message.
- **Ask your Chief of Staff**: tap **Ask anything** to give a task, ask what a coworker is doing, or create a new coworker.
- **Answer what waits for you**: the bell opens your notifications, with the questions and the approvals your coworkers are waiting on.
- **Get push notifications** when a coworker needs you or finishes a task. Turn them on or off in **Preferences** > **Notifications**.
- **Switch workspace** from the menu at the top of the home screen.

Settings that need a bigger screen, such as billing, stay in the web app at [app.rerun.build](https://app.rerun.build).
