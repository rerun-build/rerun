The Rerun app for iPhone and iPad opens the same workspace as the web app. Your coworkers, your conversations and your plan are the same on both, and a message sent from one shows up on the other.

## Install

Get Rerun from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6825274993) and open it.

## Sign in

Sign in the way you sign in on the web: **Continue with Apple**, **Continue with Google**, **Continue with phone** (a code by WhatsApp or SMS) or **Continue with email** (a code by email). Use the same account as on the web to find your workspace.

## What you can do

- **Talk to your coworkers**: the home screen lists them with what each one is doing, or the last thing said in its latest conversation (**You:** when you wrote it). Tap one to open its **General**, swipe or use the speech bubble button for its threads, and send a message, a photo, a file or a voice message. The threads open there are the ones you left open on [your other devices](/concepts#session), and **Continue** above the message box opens the one you were in; **Close conversation**, in the speech bubble menu, closes the one on screen. Pin the ones you use most to the top. On an iPad with a keyboard, <kbd>Return</kbd> sends the message and <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Return</kbd> starts a new line; with the <kbd>@</kbd>, <kbd>/</kbd> or <kbd>#</kbd> menu open, <kbd>Return</kbd> picks its first suggestion. The **+** button of the message box lists the symbols, `@` for a coworker, `/` for a skill and `#` for a computer, with what each does; your Chief of Staff has no skill, so no `/`. The on-screen keyboard's return key always starts a new line.
- **Ask your Chief of Staff**, under the name your workspace gave it: tap **Ask anything** to give a task, ask what a coworker is doing, or create a new coworker. **New coworker** creates one directly. Tap its name at the top of its chat for its card, as for a coworker: its **Files**, **Apps**, **Routines**, **Computer** and **Notifications**, and **Edit** to rename it, which takes a workspace admin.
- **Answer what waits for you**: questions, approvals and the other [pauses](/agents/human-in-the-loop#the-pauses) open as cards in the conversation, and the bell opens your notifications, your Chief of Staff's included: its conversations that wait on you, and what it finished since you last had the conversation on screen. The check at the top marks the ones that only inform you as read; the requests stay, unless you pick [Clear all](/agents/human-in-the-loop#notifications).
- **Look at a coworker**: open its details for its **Files**, its **Apps** (connect the ones that need a sign-in, or use an account already in the [Vault](/vault#from-a-coworker)), its **Routines**, its **Computer** and its **Notifications**, or to rename or delete it.
- **Pick the model**: change it per conversation from the message box, and manage the workspace's connections in **Preferences** > **AI providers**.
- **Get push notifications** when a coworker needs you or finishes a task, and when your Chief of Staff waits on you in one of your conversations or [checks in](/chief-of-staff#check-ins) with something for you: the notification opens its thread. Turn them on in **Preferences** > **Notifications**, where you also choose **Notify me about**, the email channel and the rules **Per coworker**, the same settings as on the web. See [Choose what reaches you](/agents/human-in-the-loop#choose-what-reaches-you).
  - The picture at the left of a notification is the coworker it comes from, dressed as you [dressed it](/agents/configure#appearance), or the orb of your Chief of Staff. A notification of no coworker, or one whose picture could not be loaded in time, shows Rerun's icon. On Android, a coworker that notified you is also listed when you touch and hold Rerun's icon: tap it to open its **General**, or your Chief of Staff's last conversation, in the workspace it belongs to. Signing out clears that list.
- **Answer from the notification**: touch and hold it (or swipe it left and tap **View**) for its buttons. On an iPhone each one asks for Face ID first and answers without opening the app; on Android it unlocks the phone and opens Rerun, and the answer leaves from there.
  - An approval or a spend: **Approve** or **Deny** settles the whole card.
  - A question: **Answer** opens a text field. Your words answer it, the way you would type them in the card.
  - A finished task: **Reply** sends the coworker a message in the same conversation.
  - A notification then says what was sent. If the card was already answered, in the app or with a second tap, it says so and nothing is sent twice. An app to connect, a key, a computer or a browser to take over always open the app.
- **Add the Your crew widget**: touch and hold the home screen or the lock screen, tap **Edit** > **Add Widget**, and pick **Rerun**. On the home screen it shows whoever needs you (small) or your crew at work (medium); on the lock screen, a line above the clock, three lines under it or the face of whoever waits. A tap opens the conversation. It is updated when the app is open and each time a Rerun notification reaches your phone, so it can be behind when nothing happened for a while. On Android, add the same widget from the home screen's widget list.
- **Follow your crew from the lock screen and the Dynamic Island**: while a coworker works or waits on you, a Live Activity shows who counts most, how long it has been at it, and the rest of the crew in tiles. A tap opens Rerun. It starts when you open the app while someone is at work, follows the notifications that reach you, and goes away once nobody works nor waits. Turn it off in the iOS **Settings** > **Rerun** > **Live Activities**. On Android, the same crew stays at the top of the notifications, with a **Needs you** chip in the status bar on Android 16 while someone waits; its channel is **Crew at work**.
- **Switch workspace** from the menu at the top of the home screen.
- **Open links**: a link a coworker writes opens in your phone's browser (Safari, Chrome), where you are already signed in to your tools. To keep links in the app, choose **Preferences** > **Open links in** > **Rerun**: the page opens over the conversation, and its menu can still hand it to your browser.

Settings that need a bigger screen, such as billing, stay in the web app at [app.rerun.build](https://app.rerun.build).

## Your own server

The app also connects to a self-hosted install of Rerun: tap **Use your own server** on the first screen and enter its address. See [Mobile app on your install](/self-host/mobile).

## Other apps

Rerun also runs in your browser, and in the [desktop app](/desktop-app) for macOS, Windows and Linux.
