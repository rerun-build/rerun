The Rerun app for iPhone and iPad opens the same workspace as the web app. Your coworkers, your conversations and your plan are the same on both, and a message sent from one shows up on the other.

## Install

Get Rerun from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6825274993) and open it.

## Sign in

Sign in the way you sign in on the web: **Continue with Apple**, **Continue with Google**, **Continue with phone** (a code by WhatsApp or SMS) or **Continue with email** (a code by email). Use the same account as on the web to find your workspace.

## What you can do

- **Talk to your coworkers**: the home screen lists them with what each one is doing. Tap one to open its conversation, send a message, a photo, a file or a voice message. Pin the ones you use most to the top. On an iPad with a keyboard, <kbd>Return</kbd> sends the message and <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Return</kbd> starts a new line; with the <kbd>@</kbd> or <kbd>/</kbd> menu open, <kbd>Return</kbd> picks its first suggestion. The on-screen keyboard's return key always starts a new line.
- **Ask your Chief of Staff**: tap **Ask anything** to give a task, ask what a coworker is doing, or create a new coworker. **New coworker** creates one directly.
- **Answer what waits for you**: questions, approvals and the other [pauses](/agents/human-in-the-loop#the-pauses) open as cards in the conversation, and the bell opens your notifications.
- **Look at a coworker**: open its details for its **Files**, its **Apps** (connect the ones that need a sign-in), its **Routines**, its **Computer** and its **Notifications**, or to rename or delete it.
- **Pick the model**: change it per conversation from the message box, and manage the workspace's connections in **Preferences** > **AI providers**.
- **Get push notifications** when a coworker needs you or finishes a task. Turn them on in **Preferences** > **Notifications**, where you also choose **Notify me about**, the email channel and the rules **Per coworker**, the same settings as on the web. See [Choose what reaches you](/agents/human-in-the-loop#choose-what-reaches-you).
- **Switch workspace** from the menu at the top of the home screen.

Settings that need a bigger screen, such as billing, stay in the web app at [app.rerun.build](https://app.rerun.build).

## Your own server

The app also connects to a self-hosted install of Rerun: tap **Use your own server** on the first screen and enter its address. See [Mobile app on your install](/self-host/mobile).

## Other apps

Rerun also runs in your browser, and in the [desktop app](/desktop-app) for macOS, Windows and Linux.
