A coworker that never checks with you is a liability. Rerun gives every coworker seven ways to hand a decision back, and one way to tell you something without stopping.

## The pauses

When a coworker uses any of these, it **stops its turn**. A card appears in the conversation, you answer it, and the run continues from your reply. The thread shows **Needs you** until then.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="A question" icon="message-circle-question">
    Several questions at once, each with suggested answers as buttons. A question can accept a single answer or let you tick every option that applies (**Select all that apply**). You can always pick **Something else** and type, answer with your voice, or attach up to 10 files to your answer.
  </Card>
  <Card title="An approval" icon="hand">
    **Approval required**: a list of actions it wants to take, with the detail behind each one, such as the command, the target or the diff. Click **Approve** or **Not now** per item, or **Explain** to decline with a reason the coworker reads.
  </Card>
  <Card title="An app" icon="link">
    It found the integration it needs in the library and asks you to connect it. The card carries the setup form, and offers an account the [Vault](/vault) already holds.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A credential" icon="key">
    **Credential needed**: a masked form for an API key that no app covers. **Where to find it** links to the service's page when the coworker knows it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="An MCP sign-in" icon="log-in">
    **Sign in to** an MCP server the coworker registered, so it can use its tools.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A browser takeover" icon="monitor">
    **Your turn on the screen**: the coworker needs you in its browser, for a sign-in or a captcha. **Open browser** opens its browser on its computer for you to drive. Click **Done** when it can carry on, or **Skip**. What you sign in to stays in that coworker's browser.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A spend approval" icon="coins">
    On Rerun Cloud, before it buys [leads, SEO or social data](/agents/configure#paid-data), the coworker shows the **Estimated total** in credits and what you have left. Click **Approve once**, **Always allow**, or **Not now**. **Add credits** shows when you are short.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

A coworker asks one thing at a time, batching everything into a single card, so you are never facing three stacked cards. Closing a card without answering tells the coworker you skipped it, and it carries on from there.

**Always allow** on a spend approval switches on **Spend without asking** for that coworker: it stops asking before paid data, which lets its scheduled runs and delegated tasks spend while you are away. Turn it back off in **Settings** > **Capabilities**.

Each pause comes from a [capability](/agents/configure#capabilities): **Ask you questions**, **Request approval**, **SuggestService**, **Ask for a credential**, **Improve itself** for the MCP sign-in, **Browser** for the takeover, and **Leads, SEO & social data** with **Request approval** for the spend. Turn the capability off and the coworker cannot pause that way.

## The secret guarantee

When a coworker needs an API key, it never asks for it in the chat. It opens a masked form, and the value goes straight into the coworker's variables, on your workspace's machine. It is never sent to the model.

The coworker learns which variable name was filled and nothing else. It then references it as `${VARIABLE_NAME}` in an app config or a command, and the value resolves on the machine at run time.

<Warning>
  Never paste a secret into the chat itself. The conversation is stored and sent to the model. A coworker that asks you to is doing it wrong: use the masked form.
</Warning>

## Notifications

Separate from the pauses, a coworker can tell you something and keep working. Those arrive as notifications rather than cards.

A notification is one of these kinds: `info`, something to look at, sent by the coworker's **Notify** capability; `done` and `error`, when a run finishes or fails; and `question`, `approval` and `connector`, raised by the pauses above.

Everything waiting for you is collected in the coworker's **Threads**, under **Needs you**, and in the workspace inbox.

A question, an approval or a credential can be answered from the inbox without opening the conversation. Click the update, or its line in the inbox: the card shows what the coworker wrote around its request and the same form as the chat. When a long message pushes the form out of sight, a pill at the bottom of the card says what is waiting: click it to scroll down to the form. Once you answer, it shows what was sent to the coworker and folds back. **Open conversation** takes you to the whole thread, and the arrow at the top of the card brings you back to the inbox's list where you left it. A [skill proposal](/agents/skills#skill-proposals) waiting on you shows in the same card: click **Remember** or **No** there. The other pauses (an app to connect, an MCP sign-in, a browser takeover, a spend) are answered in the conversation.

In the coworker's **General**, a thread that waits on you shows **Needs you** on its line. When it waits on a question, an approval, a credential or a skill proposal, click the line and it opens into the same card, right there: answer it, click the line again to fold it, or click **Open conversation** to go to the thread. Once you answer, the line folds back without its badge. A thread that needs you for anything else, an update to read for instance, opens in its tab as before.

## Choose what reaches you

Open the workspace menu and click **Notifications**. The settings are yours, not the workspace's.

**Notify me about** sets the rule for every coworker:

| Choice | What reaches you |
| --- | --- |
| **Everything** | Every run in the workspace, whoever started it |
| **My runs and what I follow** | Runs you start, conversations you are in, coworkers you follow. Recommended, and the default |
| **Only when I am needed** | Questions, approvals and errors in your runs. A finished run stays quiet |
| **Nothing** | You check the inbox yourself |

**Per coworker** overrides that rule for one coworker: **Default** follows your setting, **All activity** sends you every run of it, **Muted** sends nothing (the board still shows it). Set it here with **Add**, or from the bell on the coworker card, which also offers **Follow its scheduled tasks** so they reach you like your own runs. A single scheduled task or trigger has its own **Notify me** switch in its editor.

**How** picks the channels:

- **Toast in the app**: pops up above your inbox while Rerun is open.
- **Sound**: only when a coworker needs you or something breaks.
- **Email**: when a coworker needs you while you are away, one email per incident, never one per run. Coworker notification emails are paused for now: the setting is kept, and everything still arrives in the app.

**Account emails** turns **Trial & billing** and **Product updates** on or off.

The [desktop app](/desktop-app) shows notifications from your system, and the [iPhone app](/iphone-app) sends push notifications.

When the workspace's Rerun credits for the month are used up, scheduled and triggered runs on Rerun models pause, and you get one notice instead of one per failure.

## While you are away

A run started by a [scheduled task or a webhook](/agents/automations) can pause exactly like a chat run. Nothing is skipped because nobody was watching.

The run waits, the notification goes out, and the coworker resumes from your answer whenever you get to it.

## Reading it back

A paused run shows up in [`list_runs`](/api/tools/runs#list_runs) like any other, and `output` carries the coworker's last message. The card itself is answered in the app: there is no API tool for approving on someone's behalf.
