The Rerun app from the App Store works with your install. It asks your app for everything else it needs, so you only type one address.

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    Install Rerun from the App Store and open it. On the sign-in screen, tap **Use your own server**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your address">
    Under **Server address**, type the address you open Rerun at in a browser, for example `rerun.example.com` or `192.168.1.20:3000`, then tap **Connect**. The app reopens on your install.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Tap **Continue with email**. The code goes out the way your install sends them, see [Set up Supabase](/self-host/install#2-set-up-supabase).
  </Step>
</Steps>

To go back to Rerun Cloud before you sign in, tap **Use Rerun Cloud** under **Continue with email**. Once signed in, open **Preferences**, tap **Server**, then **Leave**: you are signed out, and the app reopens on Rerun Cloud.

## What the phone needs to reach

- **The app**, at the address you typed. Use `https://` on the internet. Plain `http://` only works on your local network: `localhost`, a private address such as `192.168.1.20`, a `.local` name or a name without a dot, or a Tailscale address. On iPhone, the first time, iOS asks to let Rerun find devices on your local network: allow it.
- **Supabase**, at the address your app is configured with (`NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL`), with the same rule: `https://`, or `http://` on your local network. When that address is `localhost`, as with `npx supabase start`, the phone uses the machine it reached the app on, at the same port: open that port on your network.
- **The engine**, at `RERUN_ENGINE_BROWSER_URL`, only to watch a coworker's screen.

What **Connect** says when it refuses an address:

| Message | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Use https:// outside your local network. | Put the app, or Supabase, behind HTTPS. See [On a server](/self-host/install#on-a-server). |
| Could not reach this server. | Check the address in a browser on the phone. |
| This address is not a Rerun server. | Check the address: it is the one of the app, not of Supabase or the engine. |
| Update this Rerun server to use it from the app. | Update your install. See [Updating](/self-host/install#updating). |

## What your install controls

| Variable | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `MOBILE_OAUTH_TICKET_SECRET` | Needed to connect an app by OAuth from the phone, and to sign in to a Claude, ChatGPT or Grok plan from it. See [Secrets](/self-host/configuration#secrets). |
| `MOBILE_IOS_MIN_BUILD` | The oldest build of the app your install still talks to. An older one is sent to the App Store to update. Empty: every build is let through. |
| `MOBILE_ANDROID_MIN_BUILD` | The same for the Android app, sent to Google Play. |
| `MOBILE_IOS_OPENCODE_BUILD` | The first iPhone build that knows OpenCode connections, `30` for the App Store app. While it is empty, the app does not list them. |
| `MOBILE_IOS_CUSTOM_BUILD` | The first iPhone build that knows **Custom endpoint** connections. While it is empty, the app does not list them. Leave it empty with the App Store app: it cannot add one yet. |
| `MOBILE_IOS_GROK_BUILD`, `MOBILE_ANDROID_GROK_BUILD` | The first iPhone and Android builds that know **Grok** connections. While they are empty, the app of that platform does not list them, and a coworker that runs on Grok shows as following the machine's default. |
| `MOBILE_IOS_FOREMAN_RUNS_BUILD`, `MOBILE_ANDROID_FOREMAN_RUNS_BUILD` | The first iPhone and Android builds that mark a conversation with the Chief of Staff read as they show it. While they are empty, the app's notifications list its conversations that wait on you, not the runs it finished. |
| `MOBILE_IOS_FREE_COLOR_BUILD`, `MOBILE_ANDROID_FREE_COLOR_BUILD` | The first iPhone and Android builds that draw a coworker in the color it was given, any `#rrggbb`. While they are empty, the app shows the closest of the 8 colors coworkers are born with. |
| `MOBILE_IOS_QUOTE_BUILD`, `MOBILE_ANDROID_QUOTE_BUILD` | The first iPhone and Android builds that show a [reply to a passage](/concepts#reply-to-a-passage) as a quote at the top of the bubble. While they are empty, the app shows the passage as a quoted line before the words of the reply. |

Before you raise a minimum build, check who still runs an older one: your server counts, in the `app_build_days` table of its database, one row per person, build and day the app called it.

## What is different from Rerun Cloud

- Sign-in is by email code only: phone codes go out through Rerun Cloud, and the Apple and Google buttons answer for Rerun Cloud, not for your install.
- No push notifications: they go out through Rerun Cloud, so on your install the app never asks for them. Notifications show in the app.
- On an account with [two-step verification](/account-security), the app asks for the code of the authenticator app or a backup code. Passkeys work only on the addresses the store app was built for (Rerun Cloud's), not on your install's.
