[Dokploy](https://dokploy.com) builds the same `docker-compose.yml` as a manual install, then adds the domain and the certificate. Plan for 5 to 8 minutes of build on a server with 4 vCPUs and 8 GB of memory.

## Before you start

- A server attached to Dokploy, with at least 8 GB of memory: the app build needs 6. With Supabase on the same server, use 16 GB, or 8 GB and 4 GB of swap.
- Supabase, on [supabase.com](https://supabase.com) or open source on your server, set up as in [Install](/self-host/install#2-set-up-supabase).
- Two domains pointing at the server, one for the app and one for the engine: `rerun.example.com` and `engine.example.com`.

## 1. Create the service

1. In a Dokploy project, add a **Compose** service, type **Docker Compose**.
2. Point it at your clone of `https://github.com/rerun-build/rerun.git`, branch `main`, compose path `./docker-compose.yml`.

## 2. Set the environment

Paste the content of your `.env` in the **Environment** tab. Start from `.env.example` and run `node scripts/self-host-check.mjs` on your machine to generate the secrets. Three values depend on your domains:

```bash .env
NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL=https://rerun.example.com
NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL=<the address of your Supabase>
RERUN_ENGINE_BROWSER_URL=https://engine.example.com
```

The **Site URL** of your Supabase must be the same address as `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL`.

## 3. Add the domains

In the **Domains** tab, add two domains, both with HTTPS and Let's Encrypt:

| Host | Service | Port |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `rerun.example.com` | `app` | 3000 |
| `engine.example.com` | `engine` | 3000 |

The engine needs its own domain: a browser connects to it directly to show a coworker's screen.

## 4. Deploy

Click **Deploy**. When it finishes, check both domains:

```bash
curl -s https://rerun.example.com/api/version
curl -s https://engine.example.com/health
```

Then [sign in](/self-host/install#7-sign-in).

## 5. Close the published ports

`docker-compose.yml` also publishes the app on port 3000 and the engine on port 3001. Docker opens these ports past `ufw`, so close them in your provider's firewall, and keep only 22, 80 and 443 open.

## Updating

Pull the new version into your clone, then click **Deploy**. The engine keeps its data in the `engine-data` volume. When a release adds database migrations, apply them with `npx supabase@^2.113.0 db push`.
