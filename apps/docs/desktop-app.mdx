The desktop app is Rerun in a window of its own. Everything you see is the same app as in your browser, updated at the same time, plus what a browser tab cannot do.

## Install

Download Rerun from [rerun.build/download](https://rerun.build/download). The app updates itself: when a new version is ready, a message offers to restart.

- **macOS** 13 or later: take the `.dmg` for your Mac's chip (Apple Silicon or Intel, shown in **Apple menu > About This Mac**), open it and drag **Rerun** to your **Applications** folder.
- **Windows** 10 or later, in beta: run the installer for your PC (x64, or Arm on a Windows on Arm PC). The Windows app is not signed yet, so Windows may show **Windows protected your PC**: click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Linux** x64: the AppImage runs on most distributions and updates itself (make it executable, then open it). A `.deb` and a `.rpm` are there too.

## Sign in

Click **Sign in with Google** or **Sign in with Apple**. Your browser opens on Rerun and asks you to confirm the account: click **Continue**, and the app signs in on its own.

If the app does not open by itself, the browser shows a code. In the app, click **Paste a code instead**, paste it, and click **Sign in**. The code works once, for two minutes.

You can also sign in with an email code or your phone number, right in the app.

## What the app adds

- **Notifications** from your coworkers as soon as you are in another app. Click one to open the conversation.
- **What waits for you on the app icon**: a count on the Dock icon on a Mac, a dot on the taskbar icon on Windows. The app also keeps an icon in the menu bar on a Mac, in the notification area on Windows and Linux.
- **Quick ask**: press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>Space</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Space</kbd> on Windows and Linux) from any app, type a question for your Chief of Staff, press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. The app opens on the answer. Rather talk? Hold <kbd>Space</kbd> in the empty field, ask your question out loud and let go: it is sent as soon as it is written out. Or press <kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>Space</kbd> (<kbd>Alt</kbd> <kbd>Shift</kbd> <kbd>Space</kbd> on Windows and Linux) from any app: Quick ask opens listening, press the shortcut again or <kbd>Enter</kbd> to send, <kbd>Esc</kbd> to cancel. You can pick another shortcut in the settings.
- **Open from your email**: in a browser where you signed the app in, Rerun offers to open the page in the app.
- **More than one window**: **File** > **New Window**.

On a Mac, closing the window keeps Rerun running, so notifications still arrive: quit from the menu bar icon or with <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Q</kbd>. On Windows and Linux, closing the window leaves Rerun in the notification area while **Keep running when closed** is on, which it is by default. Quit from its icon.

## Settings

Open your account from the workspace menu and pick the **Desktop app** tab:

- **Open at login** starts Rerun with your computer, in the menu bar or the notification area.
- **Quick ask** shows its shortcut. Click it and press the keys you want instead: the shortcut needs <kbd>⌘</kbd>, <kbd>⌥</kbd> or <kbd>⌃</kbd> (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> or <kbd>Alt</kbd> on Windows and Linux), and keys the system or another app already uses are refused. Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> to keep the current one, or <kbd>⌫</kbd> to turn Quick ask off; record a shortcut again to turn it back on.
- **Voice quick ask** is the shortcut that opens Quick ask listening, changed or turned off the same way.
- **Keep running when closed**, on Windows and Linux: closing the window leaves Rerun in the notification area, so notifications still arrive. Turn it off to quit when you close the window.
- **Updates** shows the version and checks for a new one.

## Your own server

The app works with a self-hosted Rerun too. Click **Change Server…** (in the **Rerun** menu on a Mac, in the **File** menu on Windows and Linux), or **Use another Rerun server** on the sign-in screen, and enter the address of your server. **Use Rerun Cloud** brings you back. See [Desktop app](/self-host/desktop).
