The desktop app is Rerun in a window of its own. Everything you see is the same app as in your browser, updated at the same time, plus what a browser tab cannot do.

## Install

Download Rerun from [rerun.build/download](https://rerun.build/download), open the file, and drag **Rerun** to your **Applications** folder. The app updates itself: when a new version is ready, a message offers to restart.

macOS 13 or later. Windows and Linux are on the way.

## Sign in

Click **Sign in with Google** or **Sign in with Apple**. Your browser opens on Rerun and asks you to confirm the account: click **Continue**, and the app signs in on its own.

If the app does not open by itself, the browser shows a code. In the app, click **Paste a code instead**, paste it, and click **Sign in**. The code works once, for two minutes.

You can also sign in with an email code or your phone number, right in the app.

## What the app adds

- **Notifications** from your coworkers as soon as you are in another app. Click one to open the conversation.
- **A count on the Dock icon** of what waits for you, and an icon in the menu bar.
- **Quick ask**: press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>Space</kbd> from any app, type a question for your Chief of Staff, press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. The app opens on the answer.
- **Open from your email**: in a browser where you signed the app in, Rerun offers to open the page in the app.
- Closing the window keeps Rerun running, so notifications still arrive. Quit from the menu bar icon or with <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Q</kbd>.

## Settings

Open your account from the workspace menu and pick the **Desktop app** tab:

- **Open at login** starts Rerun with your Mac, in the menu bar.
- **Quick ask** turns the shortcut on or off.
- **Updates** shows the version and checks for a new one.
