Your Rerun account reaches coworkers connected to your mail, your chat, your payments, the [Vault](/vault) and a whole machine. Two-step verification makes sure your email alone is not enough to open it: after your email code, Google, Apple or phone code, Rerun asks for one more step.

Everything below lives in the **Account** dialog: open the workspace pill in the top left corner, then **Account**, then the **Security** tab.

## Two-step verification

Pick one method or both. You can add or remove them at any time.

- **Authenticator app.** A 6-digit code from 1Password, Google Authenticator, Authy or any app that reads a QR code. Click **Set up**, scan the QR code (or type the key under it), then type the code your app shows and click **Confirm**. A code that does not match is refused: check the time on your phone and try the next one.
- **Passkeys.** Touch ID, Face ID, Windows Hello, an Android phone or a security key such as a YubiKey. Click **Add**, follow your browser's window, then give the passkey a name you will recognise. You can add several, rename them with the pencil and remove them with the bin.

Turning on your first method signs you out of every other browser and app, so each of them goes through the new step at its next sign-in.

Removing your last method turns two-step verification off, and your backup codes stop working.

## Backup codes

When you set up your first method, Rerun shows 10 backup codes, once. Copy them or download them, and keep them somewhere safe, away from your phone.

Each code opens your account once, when no other method is at hand. The **Backup codes** row says how many are left. **New codes** makes 10 new ones: the codes you saved stop working.

## Signing in

After your usual sign-in, Rerun asks for your second step, on the web, in the [desktop app](/desktop-app) and in the [iPhone and Android app](/iphone-app):

- The method you used last comes first: **Use my passkey** opens your device's passkey window, or you type the code from your authenticator app.
- The other methods are just below: **Use my authenticator app**, **Use my passkey**, **Use a backup code**.
- **Sign in with another account** leaves this account and goes back to the sign-in.

The desktop app signs in through your browser: the second step happens there, and the app opens already through it.

Passkeys work on app.rerun.build. On an organization's own address, sign in with your authenticator app or a backup code: the passkey options are not offered there.

## Where you're signed in

The same tab lists every browser and app your account is signed in to, with when each was last active. **This device** is the one you are reading it on.

- **Sign out** ends one session: that browser or app has to sign in again.
- **Sign out everywhere else** ends every session but this one.

## If you lose every method

If you lose your phone, your passkeys and your backup codes, write to [help@rerun.build](mailto:help@rerun.build) from the address of your account. Support checks that the account is yours before turning two-step verification off for you.
