- `serviceS`: the mean number of seconds jobs spent in the running state, over exits from it (a changing transition whose `from_state` is the running state) whose `at` is within the last `SERVICE_WINDOW_S` of `now`, each measured from the latest earlier changing transition into that state; `None` when there is no exit in the window.
